## Technical Details & Prompt Strategy

The structure of these documents highlights the implementation of **technical constraints** and **advanced prompt strategies**:

- **Voice Design Rules (TTS/STT):**  
  Defines rules for managing low-latency full-duplex conversations, including phonetic pronunciation, contact info handling, and control over natural speech fillers ("um", "uh").

- **Logic & Triage Flow:**  
  Implements a multi-step qualification and triage process, enforcing a *one-question-at-a-time* policy to ensure accurate data capture.

- **Robust Error Handling:**  
  Details fallback protocols for unsupported interactions such as IVR/Voicemail detection, and scripted responses for customer objections.

- **Persona & Tone Management:**  
  Includes explicit tone guidelines (e.g., *“Warm, reassuring”* for healthcare) to maintain brand consistency and customer satisfaction.
